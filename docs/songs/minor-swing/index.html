Minor Swing

Key: A minor 210 BPM (fast swing) Original: Django Reinhardt & Stéphane Grappelli (1937)

The quintessential gypsy jazz standard. A pure instrumental arrangement with shared melody between saxophone and piano, featuring la pompe rhythm guitar and fast walking bass.

About This Arrangement

"Minor Swing" was recorded in 1937 by Django Reinhardt and Stéphane Grappelli with the Quintette du Hot Club de France. It is the most iconic gypsy jazz composition ever written. Our arrangement adapts this classic for a swing sextet with shared melody between piano and saxophone.
